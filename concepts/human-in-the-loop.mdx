## Who this is for

Compliance teams, enterprise buyers, and admins who need clarity on human authority over AI recommendations.

## The principle

**Exterview informs decisions; it does not auto-decide or auto-reject.**

Every AI-generated recommendation is presented to a human reviewer who retains final authority. Candidates are never automatically rejected or adversely impacted without human review.

## What this means in practice

| Exterview does | Exterview does not |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Generate scores, rankings, and recommendations | Auto-reject candidates |
| Surface AI justification for each decision | Make final hiring decisions |
| Allow human override at any stage | Remove human review from the loop |
| Log overrides in the audit trail | Apply automated adverse employment actions |

## Transparency

The [Agent Transparency Panel](/guides/agent-transparency-panel) shows provenance for every signal:

- **Client**: Provided by the customer or candidate
