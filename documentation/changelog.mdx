  <Accordion icon="calendar-week" title="January 2026 (Week-3) — Weekly Platform Enhancements, Security & Improvements">
    ### 1. Dashboard & Platform Visibility

    Key improvements were made to enhance clarity and system visibility across the platform:

    - Added a **job selection empty state** before displaying the sourcing funnel on the dashboard
    - Introduced **observability dashboards** to monitor signups, interviews, and system failures
    - Enabled **alerts for error rates, latency, and queue depth**
    - Identified and tracked all backend services involved in **signup, interviews, and credit usage**

    ### 2. Customer & Account Structure

    The platform now supports stronger customer-level organization:

    - Introduced **Customers as a parent entity**
    - Enabled each customer to **own multiple jobs**
    - Added filters to **view jobs, candidates, and reports by customer**
    - Jobs are now **dynamically loaded based on the selected customer**

    ### 3. Job & Candidate Experience Enhancements

    Multiple improvements were made to simplify hiring workflows:

    - Limited candidate outcomes to **Shortlisted or Rejected**
    - Displayed a **simple rejection message** without detailed scores
    - Added **integrity check popups** to improve candidate experience
    - Enabled **Kanban-style job detail view** for better tracking
    - Added **Copy Link** and **View Report** quick actions

    ### 4. Location & Configuration Improvements

    Job location setup is now more flexible and user-friendly:

    - Enabled selection of **multiple cities** (e.g., Hyderabad, Bangalore, Others) without requiring a pin code
    - Introduced **Country → State → City configuration** for structured location management

    ### 5. Communication & Scheduling

    Interview communication has been improved for better coordination:

    - Added **calendar reminders** for interviews
    - Enabled **email confirmation notifications**

    ### 6. UI & UX Enhancements

    Several visual and interaction improvements were rolled out across the platform:

    - Replaced default job tables with a **card-based job view**
    - Replaced default candidate tables with a **card-based candidate view**
    - Updated **global navigation tabs to pill-style design**
    - Enabled upload and display of **1–2 company images** in job descriptions

    ### 7. Security & Platform Hardening

    Security and data protection were strengthened across the application:

    - Implemented **OTP lock** after repeated failed attempts
    - Sanitized **all user-provided inputs**
    - Escaped dynamic content and applied **Content Security Policy (CSP)**

    ### 8. Integrations & Event Tracking

    Enterprise integrations now offer deeper visibility and traceability:

    - Added **Darwinbox event logs** for all tenant events
    - Enabled users to **view a clear event trail** when clicking on each Darwinbox event, displayed in simple, non-technical language

    ### 9. Infrastructure & Reliability

    Platform reliability and monitoring were further strengthened:

    - Improved error detection with real-time alerts
    - Enhanced backend monitoring to proactively identify system bottlenecks
    - Increased operational visibility across critical workflows

    ### 10. Known Issues

    - Some monitoring and alert thresholds are being fine-tuned
    - Minor UI alignment improvements are still in progress

    ### 11. Upcoming

    - Advanced reporting and analytics dashboards
    - Further enterprise-grade scalability enhancements
    - Continued UX simplification across recruiter and candidate flows.

  <Accordion defaultOpen="false" icon="notes" iconType="regular" title="January 2026 (Week-2) — New Updates, Features, Improvements & Infrastructure">
    ## 1.  Core Platform & State Management

    Significant improvements were made to ensure clarity and consistency across platform states:

    - Clear differentiation between **loading, error, empty, and mutation states**
    - Introduced a **global reusable empty state component** with consistent messaging
    - Synced scores across all UI views to eliminate mismatches

    ## 2. Profile & Account Enhancements

    User personalization and onboarding experience have been improved:

    - Profile image upload enabled by clicking anywhere on the avatar placeholder
    - Mandatory **Terms & Conditions acceptance** enforced after onboarding
    - Auto-created **sample data** for new accounts (1 job, 1 candidate, 1 interview)

    ## 3. Job Creation & Management

    The job creation and publishing experience has been streamlined and made more robust:

    - Job tabs renamed to **Published, Drafts, and All**
    - Enforced job publish rules  publishing is blocked if **Location, Work Mode, or CTC** is missing
    - Visual grouping added in job creation for:
      - AI screening configuration
      - Scheduling
      - Publishing
    - Defined a standardized **score schema** (Resume, AI, Human, Cumulative)
    - Fixed score reduction issues when editing jobs post interview completion

    ## 4. Resume Upload & Candidate Intake

    Resume handling and upload workflows were enhanced for better usability:

    - New inline **drag-and-drop resume upload component**
    - Upload processing loader added within the same component
    - Resume and video upload size validation enforced via **S3**
    - Email extraction from resumes to trigger **shortlist notifications**

    ## 5. Interview Experience & Scheduling

    Interview flows were simplified to reduce friction for both recruiters and candidates:

    - Scheduling flow simplified by removing date selection
    - **Instant Interview** option enabled
    - Designed clear **Shortlisted** and **Rejected** candidate states
    - Prevented interview start when credits are unavailable or missed

    ## 6. Credits & Billing Stability

    Multiple fixes were applied to ensure accurate credit usage and recovery:

    - Fixed credit deduction issues blocking interview start
    - Credits are now released when interviews **fail or are canceled**
    - Credit usage percentage overlay added to the header for visibility

    ## 7. Voice & AI Enhancements

    AI interview experience received major voice and intelligence upgrades:

    - Voice selection option added in Settings
    - **ElevenLabs integration** enabled for intent screening agent
    - Improved AI flow consistency to reduce repeated or skipped questions

    ## 8. UI & UX Improvements

    Platform-wide visual and usability refinements were rolled out:

    - Careers page layout improved with a clean **2-column responsive structure**
    - Removed legacy split layouts and outdated guideline screens
    - Improved UI spacing across careers and dashboard views
    - Dark theme fixes applied for better readability

    ## 9. Platform, APIs & Enterprise Readiness

    Enterprise scalability and backend reliability were significantly strengthened:

    - Customer-layer APIs introduced for organization-level access
    - Auto Scaling Groups configured to avoid cold starts
    - ASG Warm Pools enabled for faster instance readiness
    - Baseline capacity set to handle **30–40% peak load**
    - ALB health checks optimized for faster failover
    - CPU and memory-based service auto-scaling enabled

    ## 10. Known Issues

    - Ongoing stabilization based on feedback from **SR, NIT, and Darwinbox teams**
    - Minor AI flow and transcript consistency improvements are still in progress

    ## 11. Upcoming

    - Further enterprise-grade stability improvements
    - Enhanced AI interview state management
    - Expanded reporting and monitoring capabilities.
  </Accordion>

    Recruiters can now modify screening questions even after job creation using the **Edit Job** option.

    - Movement now depends on the **human interview verdict**

  <Accordion icon="sparkles" title="September 2025 — What's New & What's Improved">
    **1. AI Interview Deadline Modification Process Clarified**

    Recruiters requested guidance on updating AI interview deadlines for existing candidates.

    A clear workflow has now been implemented:

    **Job → Actions → Edit Job → Update Deadline**.

    Changes apply to all pending candidates.

    **2. AI Interview Invite Threshold Configuration Added**

    AI invites were being sent inconsistently.

    The system now sends AI interview invites **only to candidates scoring above the benchmark threshold.**

    This ensures improved quality control and candidate filtering.

    **3. AI Interview Status Now Visible in Candidates Section**

    The AI interview status was missing from the Candidates tab.

    This has been addressed, and statuses are now fully visible in **Job Details → Candidates**.

    **4. Bulk Parsing & AI Interview Sending Issues Fixed**

    Users faced issues when sending AI interviews or uploading multiple files.

    Enhancements include:

    - Support for **docx, pdf, and text** files
    - Bulk upload limit increased to **20 files**

    These changes stabilize the interview sending workflow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="wrench" title="August 2025 — Feature Rollouts, Enhancements & Bug Fixes">
    **1. AI Interview Verdict Added to Reports**

    A post-interview report is now generated for all AI interviews, clearly showing whether a candidate is

    **"Recommended"** or **"Rejected."**

    **2. AI Voice Not Audible in Recordings**

    If the AI voice is not audible during screen sharing, please ensure that

    **system audio sharing is enabled**. This resolves the issue.

    **3. Human Interview Report Visibility Issue is Fixed**

    Previously, when the **Human Interview** toggle was switched off, interview reports for candidates who had completed their human interview were not visible. This issue has now been **fixed**, and reports remain visible regardless of toggle changes.

    **4. Meaning of "Not Available" Status**

    The status **"Not Available"** in the interview report indicates that the system could not generate a verdict due to the **benchmark settings** configured on the job description page.

    **5. Subtitles Support Added**

    Subtitles are now available during AI interviews for both:

    • **AI Voice**

    • **AI Avatar**

    This enhancement improves accessibility and clarity during the interview.
