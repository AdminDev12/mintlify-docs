<Accordion title="June 2026 — Microsoft Marketplace GA (Launching June 30)" icon="rocket" defaultOpen>

Everything built across January–May becomes generally available to all customers on the Microsoft Marketplace. The AI Candidate Matcher, the Phone and Voice interviewers, and the Unified Candidate Report (resume + phone + voice) are all GA at launch.

**Microsoft Marketplace — Live & Transactable**

Buy and run EX directly from the Microsoft Marketplace, billed against committed Microsoft spend.

- Transact EX directly through the Microsoft Marketplace listing
- Meter usage transparently across all billable dimensions (resume, phone, voice, avatar)
- Review usage and capacity consumption in-product
- Bill against committed Microsoft spend (MACC) — no surprise invoices

**Sales-Led Trial Experience**

A real 21-day product trial, not a sandbox.

- Start a full-product 21-day trial with one click
- Receive a Day 7 nudge as the trial progresses
- Move to read-only at Day 14 if the trial is not yet converted
- Graduate a trial to a paid plan without losing data

**ATS Report Export**

Send EX evaluation results into the ATS you already run.

- Push the unified candidate report (PDF) into your existing ATS
- Push candidate evaluation status back to the ATS
- One-way export to major ATS platforms (two-way sync — see roadmap)

**AI Avatar Interviewer — Early Access**

The video-avatar interviewer, available as a guided early-access experience at launch.

- Run a video-avatar interview as a guided early-access experience
- Capture candidate integrity consent before the interview
- Capture data-privacy consent before the interview
- Fall back to a voice interview if the avatar session is interrupted

**Interview Completion & Reliable Billing**

Reports — and charges — only ever land on interviews that were actually finished.

- Only see a competency report when enough of the interview was answered (≥ 70% of questions answered properly)
- Trust that a low score is a real result, not an unfinished interview — genuine finishers are never chased
- Automatically nudge candidates who dropped off to come back and finish (fresh session, starting from question 1)
- See live interview status in the dashboard — In progress, Follow-up in progress, Complete, Expired — never a misleading score for an incomplete session
- Get charged one credit per completed candidate, ever — incomplete interviews and recovery re-attempts are never billed
