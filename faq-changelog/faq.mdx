## Product

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does Exterview job creation work?" icon="briefcase">
    Upload a JD or give a prompt. AI extracts skills, suggests missing ones, shortlists candidates above resume score, and generates a ready-to-use JD and interview flow in minutes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I add new questions?" icon="circle-plus">
    You can add questions in two ways: edit AI-suggested questions inline, or click **Add Question** in the top-right of the questions panel to create a custom question from scratch.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I create a resume score breakdown?" icon="chart-line">
    While creating a job, navigate to **Shortlist Candidates Above Resume Score** settings. Click the settings icon to configure weightage for skill match, experience, education, work history, certifications, and resume quality.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I set the AI interview benchmark score?" icon="gauge">
    On the Job Description page, enter the minimum score a candidate must achieve in their AI interview to qualify for the next round.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I edit a job?" icon="pen-to-square">
    Go to Jobs, locate the job, click the action menu (⋯), and open the Job Description page to update any configuration.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I change the AI interview type?" icon="robot">
    Open the Job Description page, use the **AI Interview Type** dropdown to select Voice or Avatar interview, then save.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Exterview auto-reject candidates?" icon="user-check">
    No. Exterview informs hiring decisions but does not auto-reject or make automated adverse employment decisions. Humans retain final authority. See [Human-in-the-loop](/concepts/human-in-the-loop).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is evaluation data shared across customers?" icon="building">
    No. All data is strictly partitioned per tenant with no cross-tenant model bleed. See [Multi-tenancy & data isolation](/concepts/multi-tenancy-data-isolation).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is my data stored?" icon="globe">
    Data is stored in your configured residency region (IND / US / EU) on Azure infrastructure. See [Platform coverage & residency](/trust-compliance/platform-coverage-residency).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I request SOC 2 or ISO reports?" icon="certificate">
    Contact your account representative or email compliance@exterview.ai. Reports are provided under NDA via gated request.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How is usage billed?" icon="credit-card">
  Exterview bills per completed candidate evaluation. Incomplete interviews and recovery re-attempts are not billed. Usage can be transacted through the Microsoft Marketplace against committed spend.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a trial available?" icon="clock">
    A 21-day full-product trial is available. See [Changelog](/faq-changelog/changelog) for current trial terms.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
