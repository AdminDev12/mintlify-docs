## Who this is for

Recruiters and admins configuring how candidates are scored.

## Prerequisites

- A created role/evaluation: see [Creating an evaluation](/guides/creating-an-evaluation)

## Rubric configuration

Configure rubrics at two levels:

### Resume scoring

In the **Shortlist Candidates Above Resume Score** settings, customize weightage for:

- Skill match
- Experience relevance
- Education match
- Work history
- Certifications
- Resume quality

### Interview scoring

Set the **AI Interview Benchmark Score** the minimum score a candidate must achieve to qualify for the next round.

### Competency weights

Define stage, resume, and competency weights in org-wide AI screening configuration.
