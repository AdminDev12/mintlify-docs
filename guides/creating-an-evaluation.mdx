## Who this is for

Recruiters and platform admins creating evaluation workflows.

## Prerequisites

- Completed [tenant setup](/guides/tenant-setup)
- Admin or Recruiter role

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a role">
    Upload a JD file, provide a natural-language prompt, or create a role manually. The Role Agent structures skills, questionnaire, and interview flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the pipeline">
    Define evaluation stages: resume screening, phone screen, voice/avatar interview, coding assessment, and human interview rounds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add candidates">
    Upload candidates in bulk, pull from your talent pool, or receive applications via integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch evaluations">
    Trigger agent-driven stages. Monitor progress in the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
