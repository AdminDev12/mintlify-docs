## Who this is for

Recruiters and hiring managers reviewing candidate evaluation outcomes.

## Prerequisites

- At least one completed evaluation stage

## Review workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the leaderboard">
    View candidates ranked across all rounds for a job. See scores, rankings, and AI justification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the unified report">
    Review resume, phone, voice, and avatar signals stacked in one decision-ready report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Override if needed">
    Override AI recommendations at the stage level or for the overall candidate decision. Overrides are audit-logged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share or export">
    Share reports conditionally with stakeholders or download as PDF. Push to ATS where configured.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## API access

Retrieve leaderboard data programmatically via [Get Leaderboard](/api-reference/reports/get-leaderboard) and [Interview Report](/api-reference/reports/interview-report).
