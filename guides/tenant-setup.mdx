## Who this is for

Platform admins and recruiters setting up a new Exterview tenant.

## Prerequisites

- Admin account with organization creation permissions
- Selected data residency region (IND / US / EU)

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete organization setup">
    During first-run onboarding, configure your organization profile, locations, and departments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure data residency">
    Set your data residency region in compliance settings. See [Platform coverage & residency](/trust-compliance/platform-coverage-residency).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite team members">
    Add recruiters, interviewers, and admins. See [Roles & access (RBAC)](/guides/roles-access-rbac).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure AI defaults">
    Set org-wide defaults for phone screen, AI interview, and pipeline stages.
  </Step>
</Steps>
