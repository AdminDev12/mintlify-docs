## Product definition

Exterview is a **Human + Agent Workforce Evaluation Platform** for enterprise recruitment. It coordinates specialized AI agents supervised by the Smaya orchestrator to evaluate candidates across resume matching, voice and avatar interviews, coding assessments, and panel reviews.

Exterview **informs** hiring decisions. It does not auto-reject candidates or make automated adverse employment decisions.

## Mission

Exterview was built to address **volume hiring** at enterprise scale: making high-volume evaluation faster, fairer, and more auditable through AI innovation combined with human oversight.

<Card title="Our vision" icon="bullseye">
  Transform enterprise recruitment by compounding evaluation quality within each organization with strict per-tenant data isolation and transparent, human-in-the-loop scoring.
</Card>

## Evaluation pipeline

Exterview supports a multi-stage evaluation funnel:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Resume & role matching" icon="file-lines">
    Parse resumes, match candidates to role requirements, and generate structured role configurations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Voice & avatar interviews" icon="microphone">
    Run asynchronous AI-led voice and avatar interviews with consent, device checks, and full transcripts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Coding & technical assessment" icon="code">
    Evaluate technical skills through integrated coding assessments.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Panel & human interviews" icon="users">
    Support human interview rounds with AI-assisted context, fraud prevention, and structured feedback.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
