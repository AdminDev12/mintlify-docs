## Who this is for

Integration engineers connecting Exterview to existing applicant tracking systems.

## Status

**Beta** — One-way export is available. Two-way sync is on the roadmap.

## Supported patterns

### Report export

- Push unified candidate evaluation reports (PDF) to your ATS
- Push candidate evaluation status back to the ATS

### Supported platforms

| Platform | Report export | Status sync | Two-way sync |
|----------|--------------|-------------|--------------|
| Darwinbox | Beta | Beta | Roadmap |
| Phenom | Beta | Beta | Roadmap |

## Fallback connectivity

For ATS platforms not directly supported, use [Unified.to / Merge.dev](/integrations/unified-merge) as a connectivity layer.
