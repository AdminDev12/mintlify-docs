## Who this is for

| Channel | Contact |
|---------|---------|
| Email | support@exterview.ai |
| Status page | [status.exterview.ai](https://status.exterview.ai) |

## Severity definitions

| Severity | Definition | Example |
|----------|------------|---------|
| **P1 — Critical** | Platform unavailable or data integrity at risk | Complete service outage, data loss |
| **P2 — High** | Major feature degraded; workaround unavailable | API errors blocking evaluations |
| **P3 — Medium** | Feature degraded; workaround available | Single integration failure with alternative |
| **P4 — Low** | Minor issue or question | UI cosmetic issue, documentation question |

## Response-time targets

| Severity | Initial response | Target resolution |
|----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| P1 — Critical | 1 hour | 4 hours |
| P2 — High | 4 hours | 1 business day |
| P3 — Medium | 1 business day | 3 business days |
| P4 — Low | 2 business days | Best effort |

<Note>
Response targets apply to enterprise support plans. Standard plan SLAs may differ. Confirm with your account representative.
</Note>

## Escalation path

1. Submit ticket via support@exterview.ai with severity classification
2. Support team acknowledges within the response target
3. If unresolved within target, request escalation to engineering lead
4. For P1 incidents, on-call engineering is engaged automatically
