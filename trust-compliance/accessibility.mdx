## Who this is for

Accessibility reviewers and procurement teams evaluating WCAG conformance.

## Platform accessibility

Exterview is committed to WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance for the evaluation platform UI.

## Documentation site

This documentation portal targets WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance.

## VPAT

A Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT) is available upon request. Contact accessibility@exterview.ai.

<Note>
Conformance details and VPAT availability require Compliance sign-off before publish.
</Note>
