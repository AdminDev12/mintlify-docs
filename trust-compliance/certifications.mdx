## Who this is for

Enterprise procurement and security teams evaluating Exterview's compliance posture.

## Certification status

| Certification | Status | Notes |
|---------------|--------|-------|
| SOC 2 Type II | *Status to be confirmed by Compliance* | Report available via gated request |
| ISO 27001 | *Status to be confirmed by Compliance* | Information security management |
| ISO 42001 | *Status to be confirmed by Compliance* | AI management system |

<Warning>
Certification claims must be true and current. In-progress items are labeled "in progress" — never as complete. This page requires Compliance sign-off before publish.
</Warning>

## Requesting reports

To request SOC 2 or ISO audit reports:

1. Contact your Exterview account representative, or
2. Email compliance@exterview.ai with your organization name and diligence requirements

Reports are provided under NDA via a gated request flow — not as public links.
