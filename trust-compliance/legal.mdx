## Who this is for

Legal and procurement teams reviewing contractual terms.

## Legal documents

| Document | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** | Governs Exterview's processing of personal data on behalf of customers |
| **Terms of Service (ToS)** | Platform usage terms |
| **Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)** | Permitted and prohibited uses of the platform |

## Access

Legal documents are available via:

- Your Exterview account representative during procurement
- Link-out to published legal pages (URLs to be confirmed by Legal)

<Note>
Legal document links require Legal sign-off before publish.
</Note>
