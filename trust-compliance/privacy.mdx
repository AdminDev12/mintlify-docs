## Who this is for

Privacy officers, DPOs, and compliance teams evaluating data protection posture.

<Note>
This page requires Compliance and Legal sign-off before publish.
</Note>

## Regulatory posture

Exterview maintains compliance postures aligned with:

- **GDPR** — EU General Data Protection Regulation
- **DPDP** — India Digital Personal Data Protection Act

## Data subject rights

Candidates and data subjects can exercise rights including:

- Access to personal data
- Correction of inaccurate data
- Deletion / erasure requests
- Data portability (where applicable)

GDPR data-deletion requests can be submitted on behalf of candidates through the platform admin console.

## Data retention

Retention policies are configurable per region. See [Platform coverage & residency](/trust-compliance/platform-coverage-residency).

## DPO contact

For privacy inquiries and data subject requests, contact: **privacy@exterview.ai**
