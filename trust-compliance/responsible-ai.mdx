## Who this is for

Compliance teams, responsible AI reviewers, and enterprise buyers in regulated industries.

## Principles

Exterview's responsible AI approach is built on three commitments:

1. **Human-in-the-loop** Exterview informs decisions; it does not auto-reject or make automated adverse employment decisions.
2. **Transparency** Agent provenance (server / client / synthesized) is surfaced via the [Transparency Panel](/guides/agent-transparency-panel).
3. **Fairness monitoring** Bias detection, adverse-impact analysis, and audit tooling are available to customers. See [Bias / fairness controls](/guides/bias-fairness-controls).

## Bias auditing

- Automated and manual bias auditing across evaluation stages
- Adverse-Impact Matrix (AIM) for demographic fairness analysis
- Parity and drift timeline for ongoing monitoring
- Interview-level audit table with expandable reasoning

## No automated adverse decisions

<Check>
Exterview does not perform automated adverse employment decisions. All AI recommendations require human review. This statement is consistent across [Human-in-the-loop](/concepts/human-in-the-loop), [FAQ](/faq-changelog/faq), and this page.
</Check>

## Model governance

- Versioned models with audit trails
- Per-tenant compounding is offline, batched, and versioned: not live black-box learning
